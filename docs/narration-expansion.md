# Narration for the expanded reference library

On 8 November 2026 the user requested the missing narration. This authorises Andrew and Jenny recordings for 25 new references and refreshed recordings for the library index and research-source guide. These are 27 lessons per voice, bringing current coverage to 161 substantive pages per voice. The other 134 recordings, the original four pilots and optional confirmation-bias audio remain unchanged. No new teaching videos or images are produced.

The 25 scripts are individually authored adaptations in `audio/spoken/reference-*.json`, bound to the existing canonical expansion and research-source JSON. They explain the concept before a fictional example, introduce research conversationally with its limits, and conclude with a useful reflection and practical review. Each paragraph is a complete synthesis input; ordinary gaps are 900 milliseconds, topic gaps at least 1,600 milliseconds and short reflections nine seconds. Guides explain how to use the resources instead of reciting catalogues or citations. Formal references and optional longer exercises remain on the written page.

Production uses the repository-local spoken narration skill, the existing Edge voices and pacing, provider word-boundary checks, PCM assembly and measured encoded silence. Andrew remains 96 kbps; Jenny remains 80 kbps. Final MP3s, transcripts, manifests and authored scripts are retained in Git. Takes, logs, PCM and packaging caches stay outside the checkout. Technical measurements do not establish pronunciation or human listening quality; no human listening claim is made.
