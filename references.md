# Sources and further reading

These are the initial sources used in the draft, not a complete survey of the disciplines in the proposed contents. Links lead to research abstracts, publisher records or official reports. The annotations explain the role each source plays and the limits of the associated claim.

## Cultural coverage

**Henrich, J., Heine, S. J., & Norenzayan, A. (2010).** The weirdest people in the world? *Behavioral and Brain Sciences, 33*(2–3), 61–83. [Abstract and bibliographic record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20550733/). DOI: 10.1017/S0140525X0999152X.

Used to motivate scrutiny of who participates in behavioural research and how broadly conclusions can travel. A review and critique, not an individual assessment tool.

## Deception judgments

**Bond, C. F., Jr., & DePaulo, B. M. (2006).** Accuracy of deception judgments. *Personality and Social Psychology Review, 10*(3), 214–234. [Abstract and bibliographic record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16859438/). DOI: 10.1207/s15327957pspr1003_2.

Meta-analysis supporting the reported average accuracy in unaided truth–lie judgment tasks. It does not estimate the probability of deception in an individual encounter or the effectiveness of an evidence-led investigation.

**Vrij, A., Hartwig, M., & Granhag, P. A. (2019).** Reading lies: Nonverbal communication and deception. *Annual Review of Psychology, 70*, 295–317. [Abstract and bibliographic record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30609913/). DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010418-103135.

Review supporting caution about nonverbal deception cues. Used here to challenge confident interpretations of demeanour, not to claim that all nonverbal communication is meaningless.

## Investigative interviewing

**High-Value Detainee Interrogation Group (2016).** *Interrogation: A Review of the Science.* September 2016. [Official FBI report page](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/reports-and-publications/hig-report-interrogation-a-review-of-the-science-september-2016.pdf/view).

A research synthesis from the FBI-administered HIG. Used for its discussion of information gathering and rapport. Its investigative settings limit direct transfer into everyday relationships. It is a dated synthesis, not evidence that every technique associated with the FBI is effective or currently used.
