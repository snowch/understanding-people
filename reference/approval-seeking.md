# Seeking approval

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Giving substantial weight to others’ favourable reactions when making choices or judging one’s own worth.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Uncertainty, inexperience, genuine dependence or a group with costly disapproval may be involved.
