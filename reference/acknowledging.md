# Acknowledging another perspective

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Recognising the concern or experience someone expresses without claiming you agree with every conclusion.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Acknowledgement does not require accepting an inaccurate account or giving up a limit.
