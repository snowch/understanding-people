# Changing roles & life transitions

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Moving, becoming a parent, changing work, retirement or taking on care can alter what people need from one another.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

A changed role may matter, but a practical misunderstanding or an established intrusive pattern could also fit.
