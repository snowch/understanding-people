# Personality disorders: what the term means

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Clinical terms for enduring, pervasive difficulties in patterns of thinking, feeling, relating or responding, associated with significant distress or problems functioning.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Everyday traits, a period of stress and harmful conduct are not interchangeable with a personality disorder. People with the same diagnosis vary, and a diagnosis does not establish that someone is abusive. Harmful actions can be addressed regardless of whether a disorder is present.
