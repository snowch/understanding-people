# Getting support without needing a label

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

You can seek help for distress, changes in functioning or harmful treatment before knowing their cause.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

A self-help worksheet can organise an account; it cannot decide a diagnosis, treatment or the safety of a relationship.
