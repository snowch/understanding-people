# Histrionic personality disorder

A clinical condition involving a pervasive pattern of excessive emotional expression and attention-seeking, assessed in context and in relation to functioning.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Being expressive, enjoying attention, dressing in a particular way or expressing distress does not establish a disorder. Gendered judgements such as “dramatic” are not clinical evidence.
