# Children: ability, learning & expectations

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Before interpreting a child’s action as unwillingness, consider what they understand, can do and can manage in that setting.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

A difficulty may reflect the instructions, tiredness, the setting or a skill still being learned. It does not by itself establish defiance or a developmental condition.
