# Denying an event or agreement

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Rejecting an account of what was said, done or agreed. The denial itself does not establish whether the person is mistaken or being deceptive.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Memory differences, misunderstanding, shame or deception can produce similar statements. Repeated attacks on your credibility raise a different concern from disagreement about one fact.
