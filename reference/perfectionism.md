# Perfectionism & inflexible standards

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Holding standards so tightly that errors, stopping or accepting an adequate result become difficult.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Unclear requirements, fear of evaluation or a lack of skill may also drive repeated work. Perfectionism does not establish OCD or OCPD.
