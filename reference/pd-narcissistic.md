## A distinction to keep in mind

Confidence, wanting recognition, one selfish act or describing someone as “a narcissist” does not establish NPD. Narcissistic traits or behaviours are different from a diagnosed disorder. NPD is not a synonym for abuse, and an abusive pattern does not prove NPD.
