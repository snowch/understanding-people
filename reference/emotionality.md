# Negative emotionality

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

A broad personality dimension involving tendencies to experience distress, worry or emotional volatility. It is also often called neuroticism.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

A temporary reaction to stress is not enough to establish a trait. A trait description is not a mental-health diagnosis.
