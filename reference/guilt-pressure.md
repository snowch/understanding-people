# Guilt-tripping & pressure through obligation

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Using guilt or a claimed duty to press someone toward a choice, especially when their refusal is not accepted.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

There may be a real imbalance or different expectations about duties. Those can be discussed without requiring automatic agreement.
