# Fear of rejection

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Concern about being unwanted, excluded or left. It can shape how ambiguous interactions are interpreted.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Anxiety, past experiences, current losses and relationship uncertainty can overlap. This fear does not establish a personality disorder.
