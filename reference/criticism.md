# Repeated criticism

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

Repeated negative comments about someone’s actions, choices or work. Describe the comments before interpreting the pattern.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

## A distinction to keep in mind

Specific feedback requested for a shared task can look different from repeated unsolicited criticism.
