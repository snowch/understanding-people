# Emotional blackmail & conditional threats

<p class="reference-status"><strong>Stub — full entry planned</strong></p>

An everyday term for pressure that makes avoiding a threatened emotional or relational consequence conditional on compliance.

This is a starting outline. The full explanation, evidence review and practice material are still to be written.

For help with your circumstances, see [When to seek other help](../support.md).

## A distinction to keep in mind

Distress can be real even when pressure is harmful. A threat of self-harm needs an appropriate crisis response, not a promise to comply or a guess that it is a bluff.
