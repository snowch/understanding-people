## A distinction to keep in mind

A boundary is not a guarantee that another person will agree or change.

## Related entries

These are reading connections, not evidence that one idea causes another in your situation.

- [Repeated criticism](criticism.md): You can ask for a change or set a limit based on the effect of repeated comments.
- [Withdrawing from a conversation](withdraw.md): A stated limit or time-limited pause can differ from an unexplained repeated refusal to engage.
- [Trying to punish](punish.md): Consider what is communicated, the action’s purpose and its practical effect. Do not equate every refusal with punishment.
- [Review what happened](review.md): Review whether the chosen response is workable and safe, not only whether the other person changed.
