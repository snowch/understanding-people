# Contents and reading routes

The chapters follow a shared process of understanding people. Family, work, education, friendship, public life and purchasing decisions provide examples throughout; they do not define separate audiences or sections.

**Draft status:** chapters 1, 3 and 9 are available as developed samples. All other numbered chapters below are planned, with their scope shown here rather than empty chapter pages.

## Part I · Seeing clearly

1. <span id="chapter-01"></span>**[Before you explain someone](chapters/01-before-you-explain.md)** — Observation versus interpretation, competing explanations, context and the limits of mind-reading. *Draft available.*
2. <span id="chapter-02"></span>**What counts as knowing?** — Correlation and causation, uncertainty, base rates, individual versus group findings, and how to change your mind. *Planned.*

## Part II · What shapes behaviour?

3. <span id="chapter-03"></span>**[Needs, motives and competing goals](chapters/03-needs-motives-goals.md)** — Autonomy, belonging, competence, security, incentives and the difference between wanting something and being able to act. Includes self-reflection and a community-volunteering example. *Draft available.*
4. <span id="chapter-04"></span>**Beliefs, identity and personal history** — Expectations, self-image, values, learning and why the same event can mean different things to different people. *Planned.*
5. <span id="chapter-05"></span>**Emotion, stress and coping** — How circumstances affect attention and responses; why outward composure does not reveal everything someone feels. *Planned.*
6. <span id="chapter-06"></span>**Personality, habits and situations** — Traits as tendencies, patterns across contexts, habitual actions and the limitations of rigid personality types. *Planned.*
7. <span id="chapter-07"></span>**Culture, groups and power** — Social roles, norms, institutions, status, dependency and material constraints. Cultural knowledge informs questions without determining an individual's character. *Planned.*

## Part III · Learning about another person

8. <span id="chapter-08"></span>**Questions, listening and disagreement** — Open questions, checking interpretations, memory, perspective-taking and making room for an answer you did not expect. *Planned.*
9. <span id="chapter-09"></span>**[Truth, lies and uncertainty](chapters/09-truth-lies-uncertainty.md)** — Deception research, independent evidence, investigative interviewing, FBI-associated claims and the limits of behavioural cues. *Draft available.*

## Part IV · Choosing a response

10. <span id="chapter-10"></span>**Motivation, influence and cooperation** — Supporting action, explaining value, negotiating and respecting choice. Applications across learning, leadership, shared projects and buying decisions. *Planned.*
11. <span id="chapter-11"></span>**Conflict, manipulation and boundaries** — Disagreement, repeated harmful behaviour, gaslighting, coercion and proportionate protection without amateur diagnosis. *Planned.*
12. <span id="chapter-12"></span>**Putting understanding to work** — Integrating the lenses, choosing small reversible actions and knowing when further analysis will not help. *Planned.*
