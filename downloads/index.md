# Download the audiobook

Download once, then listen offline in your audiobook app. You only need one voice: Andrew and Jenny read the same material.

<!-- audiobook-downloads -->

## Listen on Android

1. Download the M4B file for your chosen voice. Keep the `.m4b` extension.
2. Put it in its own folder, for example `Audiobooks/Understanding people (Jenny)`.
3. In [Smart AudioBook Player](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ak.alizandro.smartaudiobookplayer), select `Audiobooks` as your root folder and refresh the library. Open the book to listen, change speed, resume your place or use the chapter list.

If you download another voice or edition, give that file its own book folder. There is no need to download each chapter separately.

Download before your journey, then use your car’s Bluetooth or Android Auto. These are the same authored recordings as the website, including reflection prompts and pauses. Leave writing and on-screen exercises until you are parked.

## Keep the book nearby

The [reference library](../reference/index.md), [practice pages](../practice/index.md) and source links remain available for looking things up after listening. Your reading notes stay in the browser; they are not put into the audiobook.
