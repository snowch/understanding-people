0:00 — Understanding people
Why did they react that way? Why do I keep putting this off? Understanding people explores questions like these across friendships, family life, learning, work and community life.
Read more: https://snowch.github.io/understanding-people/index.html

0:12 — 1. See clearly
Start by separating what happened from your explanation. A friend has not replied. That is different from knowing they do not care. Learn what observations and research can tell you, and what remains uncertain.
Read more: https://snowch.github.io/understanding-people/chapters/01-before-you-explain.html

0:27 — 2. Understand what shapes behaviour
Explore motivation, beliefs, emotions and habits. Ask how circumstances, culture and power affect what someone can do. Wanting to join an activity does not make transport or caring responsibilities disappear. Include yourself in these questions.
Read more: https://snowch.github.io/understanding-people/contents.html#part-ii-what-shapes-behaviour

0:46 — 3. Ask, listen and check
Practise asking useful questions and checking that you understood the answer. Explore truth and lies, including why confidence or body language cannot settle whether a claim is true.
Read more: https://snowch.github.io/understanding-people/contents.html#part-iii-learning-about-another-person

0:58 — 4. Choose a response
Then consider cooperation, motivation and boundaries. Choose a useful next step and review what happens. Where there is harm or fear, support and safety come before finding the perfect explanation.
Read more: https://snowch.github.io/understanding-people/contents.html#part-iv-choosing-a-response
