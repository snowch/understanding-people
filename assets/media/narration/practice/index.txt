Understanding people. Practice.

Choose one thing to try, then return to what happened. You can work in the book without an app or an AI account.

Work through a situation.

Use the situation worksheet to separate what happened from your interpretation, compare explanations, choose a response and review the result. It includes space for notes.

Practise a particular idea.

Confirmation bias — give a belief a fair test. The entry also links to a detailed audio explanation and a short listening exercise with saved-answer fields.

Correspondence bias — check what the situation could explain before judging someone’s character.

Declining help — compare an offer, try a response and review what changed.

Practise as you read.

Each book chapter has a compact practice section. Optional deeper exercises let you explore further without interrupting the main explanation.

The reference entries also explain how to continue in the companion app, if you have access to its private preview. Book notes and app notes are separate; they do not sync automatically.
